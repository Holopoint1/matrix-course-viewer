Getting started

Using the Course Viewer

Find a course Open Courses, pick a course, and press Start course. Use the category pills to filter.
Work through it The left panel lists every screen in order. Use Previous / Next at the top, and Mark complete & next when you've finished a screen.
Print a worksheet Any document or HTML screen has a 🖨 Print button — the print dialog also offers “Save as PDF”.
Track progress My Account shows time logged, completion and your certificate once a course hits 100%.
Resume anywhere Progress is saved in this browser automatically. Re-open a course and it returns to where you left off.
Get your certificate Finish every screen to reach 100% — your certificate then unlocks on the course and in My Account.
