Force on a Current-Carrying Conductor

A straight conductor 0.40 m long carries a current of 12 A perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of 0.8 T.

Calculate the force acting on the conductor.

2. Force on a Conductor at an Angle

A conductor 0.75 m long carries a current of 15 A and is placed in a magnetic field of 0.6 T at an angle of 45° to the field.

Calculate the magnetic force acting on the conductor.

3. Magnetic Flux Density

A conductor carrying 8 A experiences a force of 2.4 N when placed at right angles to a magnetic field. The conductor length is 0.25 m.

Determine the magnetic flux density.

4. Magnetic Circuit – Reluctance

A magnetic core has:

  • Mean magnetic path length = 0.45 m
  • Cross-sectional area = 4 × 10⁻⁴ m²
  • Relative permeability = 1500

Calculate the reluctance of the magnetic circuit.

Use: R= l/(µo x µr x A)

5. Magnetic Circuit – Magnetising Force

A magnetic core has:

  • Number of turns = 500
  • Current = 2 A
  • Mean magnetic path length = 0.50 m

Calculate the magnetising force (H).

6. Magnetic Flux in a Magnetic Circuit

A magnetic circuit has:

  • MMF = 1200 A-turns
  • Reluctance = 4.0 × 10⁵ A-turns/Wb

Calculate the magnetic flux produced.

7. Induced Voltage (Faraday's Law)

A coil of 250 turns experiences a change in magnetic flux from 0.015 Wb to 0.005 Wb in 0.04 s.

Calculate the induced voltage (EMF).

8. Generator EMF

A conductor of length 0.35 m moves at 18 m/s perpendicular to a magnetic field of 0.9 T.

Calculate the induced voltage across the conductor.

Use: E= B x l x v

9. Transformer Induced Voltage

A transformer primary has 600 turns connected to a 240 V AC supply. The secondary has 150 turns.

Calculate the secondary voltage.

10. Rotating Coil Generator

A coil has:

Number of turns = 150

Area = 0.012 m²

Magnetic field strength = 0.6 T

Rotational speed = 1200 rpm

Calculate the maximum induced EMF.

Use: Emax = N x A x B x ω

Where: ω = (2 x π x n) / 60
