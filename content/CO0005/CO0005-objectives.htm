The objectives of this course are:

  • Unit 16 of BTEC National: Electrical machines
  • To deliver the understanding of the required content in the Syllabus
  • To provide 60 Guided Learning Hours (GLH) of teaching
  • To provide an additional 30 hours of homework to give a Total Qualification Time of 90 hours

To enhance the core engineering objective:

  • MAKE is the core skill that we want to help our students become good at. For this reason there is a heavy emphasis on project work in this unit. Making skills require that students are able to integrate knowledge from different areas and apply it in a practical way. Ultimately, MAKE is what engineers do.

Teo become good at MAKEing students need to acquire a range of academic skills that are not defined purely by knowledge. We make an effort to give students to enhance these skills as they work through the syllabus.

  • Math
  • English
  • IT
  • AI

Working as part of a team is a key objective of this course. We give these skills focus with the following emphasis:

  • Developing proposals
  • Planning
  • Communication
  • Evaluation

This course is teacher centred - it is designed to show teachers how to deliver a course in Electrical Machines.

  • It does not contain all of the teaching materials needed to deliver the course: these can be sourced elsewhere, or created by AI.
  • It does contain a Scheme of Work needed to deliver this course. This includes
    • Structure of the course lesson by lesson
    • A plan that shows how the syllabus is delivered
    • Links to useful videos
    • Practical worksheets for use with Matrix equipment
    • Suggested homework
    • Suggested Assessment
    • Suggested project ideas
