The objectives of this course are:

  • Unit 16 of BTEC National: Electrical machines
  • To deliver the understanding of the required content in the Syllabus
  • To provide 60 Guided Learning Hours of teaching
  • To provide an additional 30 hours of homework

To enhance the core engineering objective:

  • Make

To enhance the Functional skills:

  • Math
  • English
  • IT
  • AI

To enhance the Project skills:

  • Developing proposals
  • Planning
  • Communication
  • Evaluation

This course is teacher centred - it is designed to show teachers how to deliver a course in Electrical Machines.

  • It does not contain all of the teaching materials needed to deliver the course: these can be sourced elsewhere, or created by AI.
  • It does contain a Scheme of Work needed to deliver this course. This includes
    • Structure of the course lesson by lesson
    • A plan that shows how the syllabus is delivered
    • Links to useful videos
    • Practical worksheets for use with Matrix equipment
    • Suggested homework
    • Suggested Assessment
    • Suggested project ideas
