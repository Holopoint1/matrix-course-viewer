Other resources

There are other places where you can look for help and support:

Flowcode has a dedicated web site with lots of information on it. You can see that here:

www.flowcode.co.uk

There are getting started guides for Flowcode and E-blocks which takes you step by step through your first program. You can see that here:

https://www.flowcode.co.uk/wiki/index.php?title=Embedded_Getting_Started_Guide

There is a fantastic Wiki with huge amounts of information and examples on Flowcode. You can see that here:

https://www.flowcode.co.uk/wiki/index.php?title=Main_Page

There is a great forum for Flowcode where you can post questions and get support. You can see that here:

https://www.flowcode.co.uk/forums/

spend a bit of time on each of these parts of the Flowcode web site and familiarise yourself with how they work.
